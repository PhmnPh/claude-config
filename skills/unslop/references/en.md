# English word lists

Source: pstack `unslop` skill by Lauren Tan (MIT). The general rules live in `SKILL.md`; this file holds the vocabulary that only applies to English text.

## AI vocabulary

Replace with plain words: additionally, crucial, delve, enduring, enhance, fostering, garner, interplay, intricate, landscape (abstract), pivotal, showcase, tapestry (abstract), testament, underscore, vibrant.

## Fancy ways to say "is"

"serves as", "stands as", "boasts", "features". Just say "is" or "has".

## Superficial -ing phrases

"highlighting...", "ensuring...", "reflecting...", "showcasing...", "fostering...". Delete, or expand with a real source and a real fact.

## Puffery

"pivotal moment", "testament to", "evolving landscape", "setting the stage for", "indelible mark", "deeply rooted". Cut it, state what happened.

## Promotional language

"nestled", "vibrant", "breathtaking", "groundbreaking", "renowned", "stunning", "must-visit". Use neutral description.

## Formulaic challenges

"Despite challenges... continues to thrive." Replace with the specific fact.

## Filler

"In order to" becomes "To". "Due to the fact that" becomes "Because". "It is important to note that" gets deleted. "could potentially possibly be argued that it might" becomes "may".

## Plain word

"utilize" becomes "use", "leverage" becomes "use", "facilitate" becomes "help", "numerous" becomes "many", "in the event that" becomes "if". The fancier synonym is rarely clearer.

## Abstract metaphor nouns

Substrate, wedge, vector, locus, vantage, nexus, primitive (as noun), harness (as metaphor), surface (as in "API surface"), bedrock, scaffolding (as metaphor), modality, paradigm, gold-plating, ratchet (as metaphor), evacuate (for moving code), endgame, north star, flywheel. Each has a plainer concrete word. "Substrate" becomes "base". "Wedge in" becomes "add". "Vector" becomes "way" or "method". "Gold-plating" becomes "more than the job needs". "Ratchet" becomes the mechanism's real name or "a limit that only tightens". "Evacuate" becomes "move out". "Endgame" becomes "the last phase".

## Chatbot residue

"I hope this helps!", "Let me know if...", "Of course!", "Certainly!", "Found the smoking gun!", "Great question!", "You're absolutely right!". Remove.

## Typography

No em dashes, no en dashes, no hyphen-as-dash, no parentheses as a dash substitute. End the sentence or use a comma. Straight quotes. Sentence case headings. No decorative emojis.
